UFLMB

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Applies to UFLMB websites and account features.

Publication check required: This notice is not yet the final GDPR Article 13 notice. Provider processing details, applicable contractual safeguards, and the retention/deletion schedule still require verification before public launch.

1. Who operates UFLMB?

UFLMB is an independent community database project operated by an individual based in Hungary. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the publisher of UFL. The operator is the controller for personal data processed for UFLMB account, support and community features.

Data controller: Krüger Róbert, an individual operator based in Hungary.
Privacy contact: uflmb.dev@gmail.com. You may also contact UFLMB through the in-site Support chat and select the account/privacy category.

2. Personal data we process

  • Account data: email address, username, authentication identifiers, account creation/update metadata and account status. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider; UFLMB does not store readable passwords.
  • Profile data: username and selected profile logo.
  • Saved preferences: theme choice, selected interface state, favourites/bookmarks and comparison selections. Some preferences are stored locally in the browser.
  • Community content: review rating, text and timestamps submitted by a member.
  • Support: support request subject, category, message content, status, sender/account identifiers and timestamps.
  • Safety and moderation: reports, warnings, moderation decisions, suspensions and administrator audit records where these features are used.
  • Technical/security data: information generated by the hosting, authentication and database services, such as request and security logs. Exact fields and retention periods depend on each provider's current configuration and must be confirmed in the operator's processing register.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating and securing accounts, authenticating members and providing account features: performance of the service contract or pre-contractual steps (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Providing support, handling reports, preventing abuse and maintaining service security: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to a documented balancing assessment.
  • Displaying and managing member-submitted reviews: performance of the service and, where applicable, legitimate interests in maintaining community features.
  • Optional website measurement through Cloudflare Web Analytics: aggregate site-performance and audience measurement. The provider states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors' personal data. UFLMB will not enable this feature until its configuration and applicable local requirements have been reviewed.

4. Service providers and recipients

UFLMB uses Supabase for authentication and shared application data, GitHub Pages and Firebase Hosting for website delivery, and may use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate website measurement after configuration. These providers process data under their own terms and applicable service agreements. The operator must maintain the current processor list, verify data locations and international-transfer safeguards, and make the relevant provider privacy information available before public launch.

UFLMB does not sell personal data and does not use advertising retargeting or cross-site advertising profiles.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The current application uses browser storage for essential interface and session functionality. Examples include the selected theme and temporary sign-in session state. These values are not used to build advertising profiles. See the Cookies & Browser Storage Notice for the current categories.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to operate without cookies. If a future feature introduces non-essential cookies or similar tracking storage, it must be assessed separately and, where required, blocked until valid consent is obtained.

6. Retention

Account and user-generated content are retained while needed to provide the service, administer the community, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Technical logs are retained according to the relevant provider's configuration. UFLMB's exact deletion, backup and log-retention periods have not yet been verified; a defined retention schedule must be approved and published before the service is treated as fully documented.

7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH) or another competent supervisory authority.

Contact UFLMB through the in-site Support chat to submit a privacy request. The operator may need to verify account ownership before acting.

8. Security

UFLMB uses access controls and its service providers' security features to protect account and community data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not include passwords, payment details or highly sensitive personal information in support messages or public reviews.

9. Children

UFLMB is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children below the age at which they can validly consent to online services under the law applicable to them. If you believe a child has provided personal data improperly, contact support.

10. Changes

This notice may be updated when UFLMB adds features, changes providers or clarifies its processing practices. The revision date above will be updated when a material change is made.
